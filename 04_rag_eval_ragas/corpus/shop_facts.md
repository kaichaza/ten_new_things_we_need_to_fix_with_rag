The Fruit Shop sells three fruits: apples at 3 per kilogram, bananas at 2 per
kilogram, and pears at 4 per kilogram.

The shop does not sell grapes, and never has. Staff suggest pears to anyone
asking for grapes.

Regular customers include Ingrid, who buys apples for baking; Kwon, who buys
bananas for his running club; and Tendai, who prefers pears. The current
customer is Matt.

Deliveries arrive on Tuesdays and Fridays. Bruised fruit is sold at half
price from a basket by the door.
