Ingrid visits the Fruit Shop every Monday and buys apples for baking. She has
been a customer for eight years and once taught the staff her apple cake
recipe. Ingrid says the shop's apples remind her of her aunt's orchard.

Kwon buys bananas in bulk for his running club, usually six kilograms at a
time. He jokes that the club runs on bananas and that the shop should sponsor
their annual race. Kwon has never bought a pear.

Tendai prefers pears above everything and once bought the entire crate before
a family gathering. Tendai often asks whether grapes will ever be stocked and
always receives the same answer: the shop does not sell grapes.
