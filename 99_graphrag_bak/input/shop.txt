The Fruit Shop sells exactly three fruits: apples at 3 per kilogram, bananas
at 2 per kilogram, and pears at 4 per kilogram. The shop does not sell
grapes because the owner considers the local grape supply unreliable.

The current customer, Matt, is newer to the shop. Deliveries arrive on
Tuesdays and Fridays. Bruised fruit is sold at half price from a basket by
the door.
