# Shop policy

The shop does not sell grapes and never has. Grapes are not stocked in any
season, and staff suggest pears to anyone who asks for them.

Bruised fruit is sold at half price from the basket by the door. This is the
only exception to fixed pricing.

Refunds are given only on the day of purchase, with the receipt, at the
till. Pre-orders can be cancelled up to one day before delivery.
